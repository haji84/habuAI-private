## A. Concepts that MUST be kept distinct

Do not turn "reconstruct" into "invent measured fixes". The output is a layered data product, not a single boolean unknown/negative:

1. **GPS_MEASURED**: genuine original fixes (latitude, longitude, timestamp, accuracy/HDOP when given) and contiguous recorded trajectory. These are observed route/time facts after QC; raw bytes remain immutable.
2. **GPS_MAP_MATCHED**: best matching public road/road-segment geometry for measured fixes, with source/version, distance residual and confidence. This is an inferred road reference, **not** a second measured fix.
3. **GPS_INTERPOLATED**: intermediate candidate location/time *only within genuinely bounded, short, kinematically plausible measured intervals* using an explicitly documented rule; never populate a real GPS raw row with fabricated points. Missing timestamps, impossible speed or ambiguous branching lower confidence or prevent interpolation.
4. **ROUTE_HYPOTHESIS**: possible path between real measured endpoints when a longer GPS segment is unobserved, only if graph/time constraints allow; may be multi-valued and must not be counted as a measured passage, active search or formal negative. Keep the actual unobserved duration.
5. **SEARCH_CONFIRMED / SEARCH_INFERRED / TRANSIT_LIKELY / INTENTIONAL_PAUSE / UNRESOLVED_ACTIVITY**: separate search-activity classifications. The user's normal search method includes **driving a vehicle**, so low/intermediate vehicle speed, repeated passes, short reversals, slow pace near roadside, route sequences, night timing, logged pauses and captures can inform SEARCH_INFERRED. No speed threshold alone proves searching; zero GPS speed alone does not prove a pause (it may be a capture stop). Log calibrated evidence and rule version with each classification.
6. **HABU_CAPTURE_POSITIVE_EVENT**: actual owner-recorded capture, an observation of Habu activity/presence at the event's **documented time and position**, subject to timestamp/location precision. This is not inferred from the absence of another observation. Keep the exact original log and event identity.
7. **NO_CAPTURE_RECORDED_DURING_INFERRED_SEARCH**: a separately useful exposure/outcome *research* signal on a reconstructed searched segment. It does **not** by itself mean "no Habu was there", and cannot be relabeled official observed_negative without the pre-existing independent active-search AND complete owner outcome scope proof.
8. **FORMAL_POSITIVE / FORMAL_NEGATIVE / FORMAL_UNKNOWN**: the unchanged existing G0/strict-selector scientific qualification states. The research/derived evidence above should be produced even when formal qualification remains BLOCKED.

This is not a proposal to abandon scientific rigor. It fixes an overly lossy intake: **UNKNOWN formal eligibility does not mean route/time/capture evidence cannot be reconstructed and used descriptively or in a declared weak-label research track.**

## C. Route/effort recovery algorithm (implement, not just document)

1. Parse original GPX trk/trkseg/trkpt and when available JSONL session records in source order. Convert real aware UTC/local times to JST and preserve native timestamp, track boundaries, accuracy, device sequence, speed and heading if supplied; validate monotonicity per segment, duplicates, HDOP/accuracy, range and impossible jumps.
2. QC measured fixes with existing guards; **do not discard a whole night because one section is missing**. Split measured segments at real source segment boundaries, owner-confirmed pauses, and credible unobserved intervals; record reasons and start/end times. A GPX logger restarting is not always equivalent to a deliberately stopped exploration.
3. In each valid contiguous segment construct a time-resolved polyline from **measured samples**. Perform road graph matching and 100m UTM cell mapping, with GNSS uncertainty and road-graph source identity. Retain original coordinates and matched candidates **only in private outputs**. Do not assume all OSM candidate roads are official, open, or accessible.
4. For measured adjacent samples, compute elapsed seconds, geodesic/UTM displacement, plausible speed and traversed 100m cells; distinguish measured endpoints, derived traversed-path cells, time interpolation and degree of confidence. If multiple road routes fit, retain alternatives / reject precise interpolation; **never claim continuous GPS across unknown gaps**.
5. For each **100m × 10-minute JST window**, aggregate evidence: route source SHA, point counts, GPS measured duration, interpolated duration, likely movement distance, slow-road effort, repeated traversal count, plausible active search seconds, road-match confidence, activity class, uncertainty and pause overlap. Repeated visits are separate effort exposures; do not collapse them solely by spatial cell ID. No measured record within a window is not automatically a zero-exposure certificate for the entire night.
6. Classify activity using a conservative, auditable rule/score or existing approved model: the **vehicle-based Habu search** pattern, speed distribution, turn-backs, repeated roadside runs, stop context, source GPS session start/end, manually confirmed interruptions and temporal anchors. Calibrate any numeric speed cutoffs on owner-reviewed labeled segments, not invented universal thresholds. Record reason codes and high/medium/low uncertainty; allow SEARCH_INFERRED useful for nonofficial research without forging SEARCH_CONFIRMED.
7. Special verified owner exception: on 2026-11-03, **22:14:53–22:44:05 JST = 29m12s deliberately paused for errands**. Classify INTENTIONAL_PAUSE, **subtract from active search effort**, leave its intervening road/path unknown as real measurement, never infer zero captures or Habu absence, and do not treat this particular split as GPS hardware failure. Do not extrapolate this classification to other nights with gaps. Preserve 18:17–23:33 as total session interval; effective elapsed-minus-pause is only an **approximate exposure proxy**, not certified active minutes.
8. Deliver actual private recovered route, segment-level audit, QC, effort class and window dataset, preserving separate source-measured vs inferred provenance. A single GPS missing segment must not cause discard of all other observed route data. Keep missing-data reasons narrow and local.

## D. Captures: the critical POSITIVE anchor

- Owner instruction: a **capture record's time is valuable evidence that Habu reaction/presence occurred around that time**. Capture records must be integrated as positive events, not lost because the surrounding 100m × 10min unit lacks a formal Negative companion.
- For each capture record, retain raw record time, local/UTC conversion, reported coordinates and accuracy, count, size/sex if recorded, event type, individual IDs and capture vs post-capture escape. Match *independently* to measured GPS fixes and/or road polyline by both time and distance; calculate Δt and Δdistance. Existing proximity criterion 100m and ±10min is a **matching tolerance**, not proof that the user saw Habu at exactly that GPX fix.
- **Time semantics matter.** Distinguish physically observed/discovered time, catch-completed time, and time a log entry was saved, if they are available. If only a capture timestamp exists, use it as the recorded positive event time with declared uncertainty; do **not** assert the snake first appeared or began moving at that exact second. An uncertain event may span adjacent 10-minute windows with a documented matching rule and no double-counting. A time-zone or logger-clock drift is an audit blocker for precise timing, not a reason to throw away the recorded capture itself.
- No nearby recorded capture in an otherwise searched window may be represented as "no capture recorded for this observed effort" for research, but not as verified ecological absence or eligible G0 Negative without existing independent proof.
- Do **not** use the night under evaluation's future capture times, eventual GPX path, discovered wetness, or event-dependent stop/movement as features for its pre-departure P0. For future-night models use **past** confirmed positive time-of-night distributions and true/precutoff weather/GIS data, corrected for route/exposure time. Later real-time P1+ versions may use only events observed *before* their own cutoff and must be immutable.
- Compare first-capture-time, hour/time window, distance/travel effort to first/second capture, direction, road category and night conditions. Keep the positive event model distinct from an overall capture-count model and avoid treating a high event count in heavily searched roads as pure habitat density.

## F. Concrete minimum acceptance tests

1. Source-hash pinned sample GPX with multiple consecutive samples produces timestamped road path, measured/inferred origin flags and correct 100m×10min bins.
2. A point sequence split by a long unmeasured interval retains **both measured route segments** and a separate unobserved interval; never fills it with invented GPS fixes or positive/negative labels.
3. November 3 confirmed pause (synthetic timestamps only) removes precisely 29m12s from session-based effort proxy and excludes overlap windows without discarding the rest of the night.
4. A real capture record at timestamp t is retained as a unique positive observation. With nearby GPS it is matched by Δt, Δm and source quality; it must **not** require negative labels to be saved. Time-only capture with missing GPS remains a positive record with unlocated/low spatial precision, not erased.
5. Capture timestamp near a 10-minute boundary honors documented temporal uncertainty. One physical event/individual is never double-counted across candidate windows or repeated GPS passes.
6. One event recording two Habu individuals counts **two owner captures** with a single event reference; Himehabu, other species and roadkills cannot increase that count.
7. Inferred slow driving may increase search-activity confidence but may **not** alone manufacture eligible Negative. A roadside stop for capture is not automatically a pause; confirmed errands pause is not automatically a Habu search.
8. An observed traversal with no capture log produces a measured "passage / no capture recorded" research record, not G0 observed_negative unless independently corroborated search and complete outcome evidence exist.
9. Repeated visits to the same cell aggregate effort correctly without multiplying a capture event or pretending every passage is independently observed ecological absence.
10. GPS clock drift, duplicate source, raw-event overlap, bad map match, contradictory record, impossible speed, missing hours/position and changed SHA are rejected or explicitly downgraded; never silently impute as measured.
11. Strict as-of: capture time/route found after a test-night P0 cutoff cannot enter that P0's features or training selection; later P1+ can use only prior events.
