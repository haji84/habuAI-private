## Hypotheses and required tests

### H1: recent rodent sighting density

- Candidate: trailing sightings per actually searched distance/time and spatially matched road window, computed strictly **before** forecast cutoff.
- Counterexample guard: 10/14 had many more recorded rodents than 10/15, but both had 3 owner captures; no reliable monotonic relationship can be asserted from four nights.
- Critical leak guard: 10/14 rodents at 20:46 and later are **after** the 20:44 Habu capture; they cannot explain a prediction issued for that earlier capture.
- Test with pre-registered temporal lags, exposure offsets, route/season/weather controls and future-blocked folds. Report missingness and observer-reporting bias.

### H2: other-serpent sightings

- On 10/15 a Himehabu was reported at 19:58 before the 20:08 owner Habu capture. It remains only a single sequential observation.
- Independent species labels: Himehabu, Akamata, Ryukyu green snake and target Habu are never merged.
- Evaluate within verified spatiotemporal buffers against actual searched Negative units; test permuted-event and no-serpent baselines.

### H3: road surface/visibility and detection

- All six October owner captures in these nights were recorded as DRY, including roadside/tree and road-center observations.
- DRY is not a no-activity rule. Do not use this as a causal conclusion.
- Separate road-surface observation from recorded meteorological rainfall; add visibility, search speed and road-edge geometry where available.
- Distinguish occurrence probability from detection probability.

### H4: site/time transfer across regions

- Candidate feature groups: elevation, slope, road class, adjacent cell, historical exposure, sunset-relative time and hotspot movement.
- The 10/14 GPX traversed a much larger geographical footprint than 10/06 or 10/08. Straight event-count comparisons are confounded by effort/route selection.
- Official Stage1 Area Top10 Recall requires an issued 1km prediction ranking; it cannot be inferred from this observed outcome log alone.

## Minimum validation protocol

1. Use only verified Raw/Gold, with Silver provenance flagged separately; no Unknown->Negative conversion without traversed-GPX evidence.
2. Join GPX against canonical road-GIS 100m segments and ten-minute time units with explicit time-bin boundary handling; do not use simple GPS grid counts as official exposure.
3. Derive each feature from information before prediction cutoff; label later observations separately as future-only.
4. Compare baseline, Champion and candidate with identical temporal folds; include calibration, confidence intervals, error decomposition and group/feature ablation.
5. Preserve ORIGINAL prediction versions. Historical reconstructed candidates are NOT eligible as official P0.
6. If valid historical forecasts or adequate folds are unavailable, output BLOCKED/NEED_MORE_DATA rather than a synthetic KPI.
7. Promote only after Stage gates and independent validation, with atomic manifest/rollback preserving prior Champion.

Four nights cannot by themselves establish statistical effects, train a reliable new Champion or certify 100m ±10min recall.
